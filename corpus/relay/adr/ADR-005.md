# ADR-005: Evaluate EKS and Lambda with two proofs of concept

**Status:** Superseded (superseded by ADR-006)
**Date:** 2026-06-17
**Author:** Timur Aliev
**Deciders:** Relay weekly sync, 2026-06-15

## Context

Relay runs on a shared EKS cluster that costs about $6,100 a month, most of it attributable to Relay, and that the platform team does not want to operate. A Lambda-based design has been proposed. Neither side has numbers.

## Options considered

- Decide on paper
- Run two proofs of concept in parallel with the same load profile and decide on cost and p95 (chosen)

## Decision

Run two compute PoCs in parallel: EKS with autoscaling (Timur) and Lambda (Sergey); decide on cost and p95 by end of July.

## Consequences

PoC-2 (EKS with autoscaling) and PoC-3 (Lambda) run from 2026-06-22 to 2026-07-24 with identical traffic. Decision criteria: monthly cost at current volume and p95 delivery latency against the 2 s SLO.
