# ADR-007: Keep Relay on EKS with autoscaling

**Status:** Rejected
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Author:** Timur Aliev
**Deciders:** Relay weekly sync, 2026-08-03

## Context

Alternative to ADR-006 put forward by the architect: keep Relay on EKS, add autoscaling and accept the cost.

## Options considered

- Keep EKS with autoscaling

## Decision

Keep Relay on EKS with autoscaling (rejected: p95 0.4 s but 1.7x the monthly cost and a second platform to run).

## Consequences

Rejected on 2026-08-03: p95 is five times better than Lambda but monthly cost is 1.7x and the platform team would have to become a Kubernetes team.
