# ADR-010: Gateway timeout policy for Lambda integrations

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Author:** Ivan Melnik
**Deciders:** Relay weekly sync

## Context

After ADR-006 the gateway timeout of 30 s was found never to apply: the gateway's hard limit is 29 s. This ADR proposes a policy for Lambda integrations.

## Options considered

- 20 s on every route, with per-route overrides

## Decision

No decision recorded; see Status.

## Consequences

Proposed, not accepted. The live value was set by the team on 2026-08-24 at 25 s; this document still says 20 s.

> Note: Never accepted; D20 (25 s) is the live decision and the ADR still proposes 20 s
