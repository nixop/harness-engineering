# ADR-003: Replace DocumentDB with DynamoDB using a single-table design

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-05-06 (proposed 2026-04-08)
**Author:** Denis Orlov
**Deciders:** Relay weekly sync, 2026-05-04

## Context

DocumentDB serves Relay's deliveries table. Read p99 has climbed to 9–11 ms at peak and cost grows with volume. Relay has six fixed access patterns. PoC-1 compared a DynamoDB single-table design with a multi-table design on a production-shaped dataset.

## Options considered

- Stay on DocumentDB and scale the instance
- DynamoDB, one table per entity (multi-table)
- DynamoDB, single-table design with composite keys (chosen)

## Decision

DynamoDB single-table design, chosen on PoC-1 results (p99 read 4 ms vs 11 ms; 38% lower cost at projected volume).

Objection recorded from Timur Aliev.

## Consequences

Single-table gave p99 read 4 ms against 11 ms multi-table and 9 ms DocumentDB, and 38% lower cost at projected volume. Every access pattern must be designed up front; a new query means a new index. The architect's objection on query flexibility is recorded. Migration is backfill, dual write, then cutover; a dry run on a production snapshot is required before the backfill.
