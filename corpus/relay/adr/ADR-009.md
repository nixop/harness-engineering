# ADR-009: Retries through SQS with a dead-letter queue

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Author:** Ivan Melnik
**Deciders:** Relay weekly sync, 2026-08-24

## Context

Gateway retries assume a synchronous backend. With Lambda, intake is asynchronous and a gateway retry can enqueue the same delivery twice.

## Options considered

- Keep gateway retries and deduplicate in the handler
- Retries in SQS with a dead-letter queue after 5 attempts; gateway retries off on /deliver (chosen)

## Decision

Retries move out of the gateway into SQS with a dead-letter queue after 5 attempts; gateway retries on /deliver are turned off.

Objection recorded from Anna Sokolova.

## Consequences

Supersedes the Retries section of ADR-002. Exponential backoff comes from the queue; the retry budget is no longer needed. The core lead's objection (turning gateway retries off before the queue is load-tested) is recorded.
