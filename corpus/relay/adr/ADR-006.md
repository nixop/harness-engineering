# ADR-006: Run Relay on Lambda with provisioned concurrency

**Status:** Accepted, supersedes ADR-005
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Author:** Ivan Melnik
**Deciders:** Relay weekly sync, 2026-08-03

## Context

PoC-3 measured p95 1.9 s with provisioned concurrency and 3.4 s without; monthly cost $3,600, 41% below EKS. PoC-2 measured p95 0.4 s at $6,100 and a second platform to operate.

## Options considered

- EKS with autoscaling (ADR-007, rejected)
- Lambda with provisioned concurrency on /deliver (chosen)

## Decision

Relay moves to Lambda with provisioned concurrency on /deliver; PoC-3 showed 41% lower monthly cost and p95 1.9 s with warm instances.

Objection recorded from Timur Aliev.

## Consequences

Supersedes ADR-005. Provisioned concurrency on /deliver is a condition, not an optimisation: without it the SLO is missed. A load test at 10x current volume is required before cutover. Retries and timeouts have to be redone for an asynchronous runtime (see ADR-009). The architect's objection on latency is recorded.
